## Video idea #1: "The Lights Come On" (Reel, 15–25 s, vertical)

**Film:** phone on a tripod, vertical, framed like shot #2. Start recording with the set lights **off** and the room dim. After 3 s, switch the tree lights on. Hold for 5 s. Then a slow walk-in (handheld or a slow push) toward the couch for 8–10 s. Film 3 takes.

**Brief for the McGuire editing agent:** Short-form vertical reel, 15–25 s. Cut the best take: dark set → lights-on moment (cut exactly on the switch) → slow push-in. Add a soft upbeat Christmas music bed, light grade (warm, keep the whites white). Text on screen in Title Case, no em dashes: 0–3 s "The Christmas Set", lights-on "Is Up 🎄", last 3 s "Holiday Minis · Link In Bio". Export 1080×1920.

## Video idea #2 (optional): "Hi, I'm Andre" (Reel, 30–40 s, talking head)

**Film:** you on the couch on the set, phone at eye level, vertical. Say in your own words: who you are, that Studio 404 is a photo studio on St. Laurent, that the Christmas set is up, minis are $125 for 30 minutes, and the truck is collecting warm clothes. One take is fine; mistakes get cut.

**Brief for the McGuire editing agent:** Talking-head short, cut out filler and pauses (semantic cuts), burned-in captions, two or three B-roll cutaways from shots 4–8 and the truck. End card: "Holiday Minis · Link In Bio". Export 1080×1920.
